Read this first. Every number here is a population-level estimate, and many are extrapolations from analogues or from user-reported durations rather than measured human data — each value is labelled with its confidence. Individual metabolism varies enormously (CYP genotype alone can change a half-life tenfold). This is a personal tracking and harm-reduction reference, not medical advice, not a dosing authority, and not a substitute for a clinician or poison control. An absent interaction warning means "not in this database", never "safe".

Your log is stored only in this browser's local storage. The app makes no network requests.

Offline single-page app. Data drawn from published pharmacokinetic literature, DrugBank, PubChem, EMCDDA reports and harm-reduction references; per-entry sources are listed on each substance page.

In an emergency, call your local emergency number. US Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222. Recovery position for anyone unconscious. Naloxone reverses opioid overdose only.
